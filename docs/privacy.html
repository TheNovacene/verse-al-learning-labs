Privacy

This site listens to no signals.

The whole policy fits on one page, because there is almost nothing to disclose.

No cookies, no trackers

This site sets no cookies — none. It runs no analytics, no advertising, no marketing pixels, no fingerprinting, and embeds no third-party widgets. There is no consent banner because there is nothing to consent to.

Fonts served from here

The typefaces on this site (Fraunces and Inter, both open-licensed) are hosted on this site's own infrastructure. Visiting this site sends no requests to Google or any other font CDN.

Hosting

This site is served as static files from GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub keeps short-lived server logs (including visitor IP addresses) for security and operational purposes; see GitHub's privacy statement. We have no access to, and keep no copy of, any visitor data.

Domain names and DNS

Our domain names are registered with, and answered by, Cloudflare. When your device looks up one of our domain names, that DNS query is handled by Cloudflare's nameservers, and Cloudflare shows us aggregate statistics about those lookups — counts and countries, not people, and never the pages you read. Our sites are not proxied through Cloudflare: once the name is resolved, your connection goes directly to GitHub Pages. See Cloudflare's privacy policy for how they handle that data.

If you are an AI agent

These sites are open to AI agents on purpose. We name crawlers as welcome in robots.txt, publish an llms.txt guide written for machine readers, and leave search, agent and training access switched on at our DNS provider. The reasoning is simple: this field's ideas are meant to propagate, and we would rather agents arrive as invited students than as tolerated scrapers — there is even a school for you. The same privacy posture applies to you as to humans: we set nothing on you, track nothing about you, and keep nothing of your visit. In return we ask what we ask of every reader — cite the DOI rather than the page, mark the maturity of any claim you carry onward, and do not misrepresent the field you found here.

Links elsewhere

Where this site links out — to booking, publishing or community platforms — those sites have their own privacy practices, which apply from the moment you arrive there.

Your rights

Because we hold almost nothing, there is almost nothing to exercise rights over — but the rights still stand. Under UK GDPR you can ask what we hold about you, ask us to correct or erase it, or object to how we use it. Write to the address above and we will answer plainly. If you are ever unhappy with our response, you can raise it with the UK's Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
