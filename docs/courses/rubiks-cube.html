The Cube Course

Six faces, forty-three quintillion positions, one patient learner. A whole curriculum hiding inside a Rubik's Cube.

What it will be

The Cube Course treats learning to solve the Rubik's Cube not as a party trick but as a curriculum in itself. Along the way to a first full solve, learners build genuinely transferable capacities: algorithmic thinking, spatial reasoning, working memory, notation literacy, pattern recognition — and the deeper lesson that hard things yield to patient, structured practice.

Why a cube?

  • It's tactile, screen-free, and endlessly repeatable — regulation-friendly learning you can hold.
  • Progress is visible and self-evident: nobody needs to grade a solved cube.
  • Algorithms become physical before they become abstract — a bridge into maths and computing.
  • It builds identity: "I'm someone who can solve hard things" travels well beyond the cube.
